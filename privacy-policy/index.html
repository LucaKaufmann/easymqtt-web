Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026. This policy covers the EasyMQTT apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

The short version

  • EasyMQTT has no accounts and shows no ads. It does not track you across other apps or websites, and your data is never sold.
  • Your brokers, credentials, topics and messages stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. They are never sent to us.
  • The app sends anonymous usage analytics and, from version 1.27.1, anonymous crash and hang reports. You can turn both off in Settings.
  • Purchases of EasyMQTT Plus are handled by Apple, with RevenueCat managing your subscription status.

Who we are

EasyMQTT is made by Luca Kaufmann, an independent developer in Switzerland. For any privacy question or request, write to support@easymqtt.app.

Your MQTT data

The app connects directly from your device to the brokers you set up. Broker settings, usernames, passwords, client certificates, saved favorites, graphs, widgets and the messages you send and receive are stored on your device. We do not run a server between you and your brokers, and we never receive your topics, payloads, broker addresses or credentials.

iCloud sync

If you use iCloud, the app syncs your brokers, certificates, favorites, graphs and widgets between your devices through your private iCloud database, using Apple's CloudKit. This includes the broker passwords you save. Your private iCloud data belongs to your Apple Account, and we cannot read it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Location

Location is optional. If you save a location for a broker, or use features that pick the nearest saved broker, the app uses your device's location on the device to do that. Saved broker locations are stored with the broker and sync through your iCloud like the rest of your broker settings. Your location is never sent to us. When the app suggests a nearby broker, analytics records only a rough distance band, never where you are. You can turn location access off at any time in your device's settings.

Anonymous analytics

To understand which features are used and where the app fails, EasyMQTT sends anonymous usage signals to TelemetryDeck. Analytics is on by default, and you can turn it off in the app's Settings at any time.

Signals describe app events, such as the app opening, a feature being used or a connection failing, together with non-identifying details such as the app version, the operating system version, the MQTT version and protocol in use, and whether TLS or a client certificate is enabled. Signals never contain your topics, payloads, broker addresses, usernames or passwords. TelemetryDeck identifies a device only by an anonymous, hashed identifier, so we cannot tell who you are.

Crash and hang reports

From version 1.27.1, when analytics is on, the app forwards crash and hang reports that Apple's MetricKit collects on your device. A report contains technical details only, such as the app version and build, the operating system version and the type of error. It is sent through TelemetryDeck under the same anonymous identifier, and it stops when you turn analytics off.

Purchases

EasyMQTT Plus is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details. To unlock Plus and keep your subscription status in step across devices, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase and subscription status from Apple. When analytics is on, the anonymous TelemetryDeck identifier is attached, so purchase and usage statistics can be compared. Turning analytics off removes it.

Widgets, Shortcuts and Siri

Widgets, Shortcuts and Siri actions run on your device and use the same data as the app. From version 1.27.1, broker passwords and connection details are not offered to Shortcuts or Siri.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off analytics and crash reports in the app's Settings.
  • Remove synced data by deleting the app's data from iCloud in your device's settings, then deleting the app.
  • Ask about your data, or ask us to delete anything we hold, by writing to support@easymqtt.app. Because analytics is anonymous, we may not be able to link it to you.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection or the EU General Data Protection Regulation, including access, correction and deletion.

Children

EasyMQTT is a technical tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
